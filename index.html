03Philosophy

Effective altruismdrives us

At the heart of Founder Ventures is the philosophy of effective altruism. Effective altruism is a guiding principle that involves strategically and ethically maximizing the impact of one's efforts to alleviate suffering and make the world a better place. We carefully evaluate and select ventures that align with this philosophy, ensuring that every investment contributes to positive and sustainable change.

Nonprofit model

Founder Ventures operates as a nonprofit venture capitalist fund that is managed by philanthropic donors. In a departure from traditional models, any generated returns are systematically reinvested, sustaining ongoing support for projects tackling societal and global issues.
